The Old Testament reading text renders the Hebrew divine name as **Yahweh**, and the distinct short form H3050 as **Yah**. New Testament restorations are limited to manually approved quotations whose Greek wording can be traced through the Septuagint to a Hebrew divine-name locus.

The clean reading text carries no local markers. This appendix records every approved New Testament restoration and the evidence used to approve it. The source datasets are pinned to `brain-fuel/bible` commit `94d44842cb242e8aa840330748e03d2803f2a7c1`.

## Matthew 3:3

- **English token:** Lord
- **Septuagint witness:** Isaiah 40:3, *κυρίου*
- **Masoretic witness:** Isaiah 40:3, H3068
- **Classification:** Direct quotation
- **Review state:** Approved

The Greek formula quotes Isaiah 40:3, where the corresponding Hebrew locus reads YHWH. The reading text therefore has: “Prepare ye the way of Yahweh.”

## Matthew 4:10

- **English token:** Lord
- **Septuagint witness:** Deuteronomy 6:13, *κύριον*
- **Masoretic witness:** Deuteronomy 6:13, H3068
- **Classification:** Adapted quotation
- **Review state:** Approved

Jesus’ formulaic citation preserves the divine-name locus despite verbal adaptation. The reading text therefore has: “Thou shalt worship Yahweh thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.”

## Matthew 4:7

Jesus quotes Deuteronomy 6:16, whose corresponding Hebrew locus contains H3068. The reading text therefore has: “Thou shalt not tempt Yahweh thy God.”

## Matthew 22:37

Jesus quotes the command in Deuteronomy 6:5 to love YHWH. The reading text therefore has: “Thou shalt love Yahweh thy God with all thy heart.”

## Luke 10:27

The lawyer recites the same command from Deuteronomy 6:5. The reading text therefore has: “Thou shalt love Yahweh thy God with all thy heart.”

## Matthew 22:44

- **English token:** Lord
- **Septuagint witness:** Psalm 109:1, *κύριος*
- **Masoretic witness:** Psalm 110:1, H3068
- **Classification:** Direct quotation
- **Review state:** Approved

The first “Lord” corresponds to YHWH; the second is the distinct messianic title. The reading text therefore has: “Yahweh said unto my Lord.”
